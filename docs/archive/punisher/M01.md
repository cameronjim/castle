# M01: Cell Block D

Data asset: `DA_M01_CellBlockD`. Map: `L_M01_CellBlockD`. Flashback after: `DA_FB01_Sunday`.
Target play time: 8 to 12 minutes. Status: greybox built, not yet tuned by a human.

## Beats
1. Frank wakes on the floor of his cell. No weapon, no HUD objective text, just the cell.
2. The cell door is open a crack (someone left it, or something failed). He walks out.
3. Corridor 1: two guards on short patrol loops. One carries the pistol and the keycard.
4. Frank drops the first guard from behind, takes the pistol and keycard.
5. Guard station: a locked door with a card reader. The keycard opens it.
6. Corridor 2 into the infirmary hallway: three guards. This is the first fight.
7. Exit room and stairwell. Frank reaches the stairs.
8. End card, then Flashback 1 "Sunday".

## Guards
Five total, all `BP_Guard`, pistol at 12 damage. Sight 1500 units, 35 degree half angle.
Hearing 1200. Only the first corridor guard drops loot.

## Objectives (`DA_M01_CellBlockD`)
| Id | Title | Completed by |
|----|-------|--------------|
| `leave_cell` | Get out of the cell | Trigger volume outside the cell |
| `find_weapon` | Find a weapon | Picking up the pistol |
| `security_door` | Get through the security door | Opening `BP_Door_Keycard` |
| `reach_stairwell` | Reach the stairwell | Trigger volume in the exit room |

Order not enforced. Objective text hidden (stage 3 flag, currently on for testing).

## Frank's lines (placeholder, none recorded)
- On waking: nothing. Silence for the whole cell.
- On first takedown: "Sorry."
- On pistol pickup: nothing. The ammo counter appearing is the line.
- On door open, looking down corridor 2: "Infirmary."
- End card: "They kept me alive for a reason. I'm going to find out what."

## What the player learns
Movement, sprint, crouch. That guards hear sprinting. Takedown from behind. Pistol
handling. Keycards open doors. That the objective is a place, not a kill count.

## Direction notes (2026-09-18, after third play)
- **Pistol comes at the end of the level, not from the first guard.** Frank is unarmed for
  most of Cell Block D: stealth, takedowns, fists. The pistol is the reward for reaching the
  guard station or the infirmary. Cameron wants the game "relatively hard-ish"; difficulty
  comes from being unarmed, not from tougher guards. Not implemented yet; gameplay systems
  first, then this story and layout change.
- Hotbar and inventory: slots for hands, pistol, rifle (later). Inventory shows everything
  carried (weapons, keycards, ammo) and clears at the end of each mission.
- The guard station should be as lit and finished as the cell and corridor.
- Frank should hold the gun lower, and aiming should bring it less far forward.
- Crosshair much smaller, smaller gap.

## Open visual notes (2026-09-18, from renders)
- Aim pose sits low; the slide should meet the crosshair. Limited by the mannequin's reach
  from a shoulder behind the camera. A real first-person arms pack from Fab fixes this
  properly.
- Guards read as bare mannequins in brighter light. Need helmet, vest, and a stronger
  silhouette to land "black-site security."
- Keycard door slab used the engine checkerboard placeholder; steel material queued.

## Lighting intent
Dark but seeable. Cameron's call after the second play (2026-09-18): the first pass at
-4.5 EV was too dark to test. Shipped look is -3.0 EV exposure compensation on `PP_Global`;
a testing preset of -1.5 EV is one flag away (`CASTLE_BRIGHT=1`, or `create-content.ps1
-Bright`). Tubes and emissives scale with the preset so they don't blow out. Every later
room uses the same preset so the game reads consistently.
