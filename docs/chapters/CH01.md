# CH01: Rooftops

Data asset: `DA_CH01_Rooftops`. Map: `L_District_EastVillage` (game mode override
`BP_GameMode_EastVillage`). Playable: Kate. Flashback after: none yet (CH02 Carnival is the
flashback chapter). Target play time: 10 to 15 minutes. Status: blocked out as a traversal
test; no story beats or enemies placed yet.

## Purpose
The tutorial and the thesis: moving through this city is fun. Kate on a self-assigned
patrol, learning the block, and ending on an arrow that isn't hers.

## Beats (target)
1. Kate on a street by Tompkins Square Park, evening, lamps on. A text from Grills about a
   Tracksuit sighting nearby. Walk, run, sprint, slide taught by the route.
2. First rooftop: fire escape or a mantle chain up a tenement. Vault, mantle, ledge.
3. Cross the block along the roofs without touching the street. Grapple arrow introduced
   at the first gap that can't be jumped.
4. A Tracksuit pair on a roof, arguing. First fight: fists and the bow's melee. First
   takedown from behind.
5. Their target: a purple-fletched arrow pinned through a note into a water tower. Not
   Kate's. Not Clint's. End card: "Okay. That's not one of mine."
